The design and styling rules for X-Tech and everything related to it: logos, artwork, icons, styling
and branding. The logo, icon and icon pack files live in the
[branding folder](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/.github/tree/main/branding) of the
organisation's `.github` repository.

## Fonts

- [Rajdhani](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Rajdhani) Bold, by Indian Type Foundry, for the logo and headings.
- [Noto Sans](https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans) for text.

Both are free under the SIL Open Font License.

## App icons

Every X-Tech app has its own icon, drawn in the X-Tech style. No app ships the default icon of a template or toolkit (Electron, Capacitor, Vite) or an icon from another project.

- The icon is the X-Tech hexagon, flat top and bottom, in Baby Blue `#0088ff`, with a glyph for what the app does cut out of it, so the background shows through: a tick for X-Tasks, a robot head for CODEX, the Nix snowflake for X-Nix.
- The glyph is one plain, solid shape, centred, at the same margin and line weight as the other app icons. No text, no gradients, no shadows and no other colours.
- The source is an SVG with a `viewBox` of `0 0 64 64`, the hexagon `M63 32 47.5 58.847 16.5 58.847 1 32 16.5 5.153 47.5 5.153Z`, and a `<title>` with the app's name. Where it can, the cut-out goes into the same path with `fill-rule="evenodd"`, so Android's vector icons can draw it too.
- The SVG lives at `resources/icon.svg` in the app's repo. Every other size is generated from it, never drawn again by hand: the desktop icon (`resources/icon.png`, 512 by 512), the Android launcher icons, and the web page's favicon.
- A new app's icon is drawn when the app is made. An app without one gets one the next time it is touched.

## Naming scheme

- Every X-Tech item begins with an `X` and a hyphen, followed by a descriptive word for the entity (X-Notes, X-Apps, X-Security).
- More than one word may be used to tell names apart. Each additional word is prefaced by a hyphen (X-Device-Management-Interface, X-Security-Software).
- A name of more than one word may be shortened to an acronym if that is easier to say (X-Device-Management-Interface becomes XDMI).
- Where a hyphen cannot be used, because of programming or filename limits, the hyphen is dropped and the name is camel case (XNotes).
