The design and styling rules for X-Tech and everything related to it: logos, artwork, icons, styling
and branding. The logo, icon and icon pack files live in the
[branding folder](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/.github/tree/main/branding) of the
organisation's `.github` repository.

## Fonts

- [Rajdhani](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Rajdhani) Bold, by Indian Type Foundry, for the logo and headings.
- [Noto Sans](https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans) for text.

Both are free under the SIL Open Font License.

## Colour scheme

| Name      | Hex       | Use                                  |
| --------- | --------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Baby blue | `#0088ff` | The brand colour: logo, icon, links. |
| Sky blue  | `#00acff` | The icon pack.                       |
| White     | `#ffffff` | Backgrounds and the X in the icon.   |

## Naming scheme

- Every X-Tech item begins with an `X` and a hyphen, followed by a descriptive word for the entity (X-Notes, X-Apps, X-Security).
- More than one word may be used to tell names apart. Each additional word is prefaced by a hyphen (X-Device-Management-Interface, X-Security-Software).
- A name of more than one word may be shortened to an acronym if that is easier to say (X-Device-Management-Interface becomes XDMI).
- Where a hyphen cannot be used, because of programming or filename limits, the hyphen is dropped and the name is camel case (XNotes).
