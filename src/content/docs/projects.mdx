## X-Backups

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/X-Backups.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**Borg backups for all your machines, from one web page.** A small agent on each host runs every
backup, prune, check and compact on schedules X-Backups keeps itself. The page shows how fresh,
large and healthy each backup is, maps what is backed up where, and lets you browse, compare,
download and restore archives.

**Status: usable.** Users and roles, single sign-on, a health API for X-Monitor and Prometheus, and
NixOS modules for the server and the agent all work. Creating repositories from the page,
notifications and cloud sync are still to come.

![The X-Backups overview, with health tiles and a map of sources, jobs and repositories](/screenshots/x-backups.png)

[Documentation](https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/X-Backups/) ·
[Source code](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/X-Backups)

## X-Budget

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/X-Budget.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**A budget you plan once and measure every month.** Plan income and expense lines nested as deep as
you like, record each month's transactions, and see how the month measured up, with what stayed
within the plan kept apart from what went off budget. It runs on the web, on the desktop and on
Android; the apps work offline and sync through your own server.

**Status: work in progress.** The plan, transactions, monthly report, history with restore, several
budgets and import from the Trilium Budget add-on work. There are no released builds yet.

![The X-Budget report for a month, with income, spending and what went off budget](/screenshots/x-budget.png)

[Source code](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/X-Budget)

## X-Fitness

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/X-Fitness.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**Workouts, diet and body measurements in one app.** Log workouts with programs, sets and a rest
timer; keep a food diary with around 80 nutrients against your targets; and track body metrics
with goals, trends and a 3D body shaped to your readings. It runs on the web, on the desktop and on
Android, and syncs through your own server.

**Status: usable.** Workouts, diet, body tracking, training calculators, sync and a version history
for every item all work.

![The X-Fitness overview, with today's energy and protein against targets and workouts per week](/screenshots/x-fitness.png)

[Source code](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/X-Fitness)

## X-Libs

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/X-Libs.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**The shared library every X-app is built on.** One npm package, `@x-tech-laboratories/x-libs`,
with about a hundred modules: accounts and sign-in, SQLite sync, history and backups, plugins,
Electron and Android helpers, a set of React widgets drawn with the Halon theme, and the framework
this website is built with. When one app builds something another could use, it moves here.

**Status: usable.** It is released and used by every X-app. More pieces from the apps are moving in.

[Source code](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/X-Libs)

## X-Login

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/X-Login.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**A LightDM login screen drawn as a web page.** X-Login builds on

in the X-Tech style is still to come.

## X-Media-Tracker

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/X-Media-Tracker.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**Your movies and TV shows, watched and still to watch.** Keep what you plan to watch, are

**Status: usable.** The library, title pages, episode tracking, collections, imports and sync all

## X-Monitor

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/X-Monitor.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**A network and system monitor for Linux.** X-Monitor watches your machines, homelab and small
network from one dashboard, and can act on what it watches: restart a service, reboot a host or
wake it over the network. Hosts you control run an optional agent that streams events as they
happen; routers, switches and appliances are watched without one, over SSH, HTTP, DNS and ICMP.

**Status: usable.** Monitors are plugins, logs from every host can be searched at once, and results
reach you through notifications, Prometheus, InfluxDB and MQTT. X-Monitor was called Vigil.

![The X-Monitor overview on a demo fleet](/screenshots/x-monitor.png)

[Source code](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/X-Monitor)

## X-Music

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/X-Music.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**A desktop music player with a real library engine.** X-Music brings together gmusicbrowser's data
model and power with a user interface people can re-layout and re-skin, and imports an existing
gmusicbrowser library. A track is a piece of music, not a file: it survives renames, moves, format
changes and retagging, so your ratings and play history never get lost.

**Status: work in progress.** The library, filtering, playback and gmusicbrowser import work; tag
writing, media keys and smart playlists are still to come.

![The X-Music main window, with the player, filter panes and song list](/screenshots/x-music.png)

[Source code](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/X-Music)

## X-Nix

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/X-Nix.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**NixOS deployments across a fleet of machines, from one web page.** X-Nix shows which hosts have
drifted from their flake, then updates, builds and switches them, with a closure diff before each
switch, health checks and automatic rollback. It also cleans the Nix store, manages boot entries,
and builds for and pushes to an Attic binary cache.

**Status: usable.** Flakes, deployments, fleet runs, caches and provisioning work. Testing
wake-on-LAN on real hardware is still to come.

![The X-Nix overview, with host status and a map of which flakes build which hosts](/screenshots/x-nix.png)

[Source code](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/X-Nix)

## X-Plymouth

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/X-Plymouth.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**A NixOS boot splash that pulses the NixOS logo.** X-Plymouth comes in a light and a dark scheme

**Status: usable.** It installs as a NixOS module, with or without flakes.

## X-Tasks

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/X-Tasks.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**A to-do manager with projects, habits and rule tags.** Projects are tasks with tasks inside them,
tags can follow rules, habits keep streaks, and every task keeps its history. Look at your tasks
as a list, table, board, calendar or Gantt chart. It runs on the desktop and on Android.

**Status: usable.** Installers for Linux and an Android build are released on every change.

![All tasks in one list, with projects as nested tasks and tags on every row](/screenshots/x-tasks.png)

[Documentation](https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/X-Tasks/) ·
[Source code](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/X-Tasks)
